# BiRAGAS Cancer Optimizer

A 3D, patient-facing website for people whose **melanoma or advanced solid tumor has stopped
responding to therapy** (resistance / non-response). It tells the story of the BiRAGAS journey:

1. **Sequence** — NGS transcriptome analysis of the patient's tumor tissue.
2. **Model** — data enters BiRAGAS, a causal-inference agentic-AI bioinformatics platform (10 modules,
   conditional-independence skeleton, not correlation).
3. **Find the cause** — a certified causal DAG + in-silico perturbation identify the causal pathway
   driving drug resistance.
4. **Validate** — placebo/bootstrap refutation + honest abstention.
5. **Direct** — tailored therapeutic options matched to the patient's resistance mechanism.

## Tech
- Self-contained `index.html` (CDN: Three.js r128 for the WebGL DNA-helix/causal-network hero, Manrope font).
- Glassmorphism, 3D-tilt cards, scroll-reveal, interactive correlation→causation canvas.
- Deep-space navy + bioluminescent teal + gold palette.

## Grounding (real, cited)
Six resistance patterns and the 329-trial evidence from `BiRAGAS_Failed_Drugs(C)` (ClinicalTrials.gov,
PubMed, openFDA). Engine (7 stages, 10 modules) from the BiRAGAS Deployed Engine Field Guide. No
outcomes fabricated. Research / decision-support only; not a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
