You are not out of options

A therapy that stops working is information, not a verdict.

Resistance and non-response are rarely bad luck. When melanoma or an advanced solid tumor escapes a drug, it usually does so through a specific, readable biological mechanism, a pathway it switched on, a target that was never really the driver, a route it rerouted around.

The same biology that explains why a treatment failed can point toward one that won't. We read that biology, causally, and hand your oncologist a direction grounded in cause.

The causal pathway driving resistance

BiRAGAS assembles a certified causal DAG and runs in-silico perturbations to identify the causal pathway your tumor is using to escape the therapy, the mechanism, not a correlate.

The core distinction

Resistance is a causal question.

A correlation map is a hairball, and the node your drug hits may be a passenger. BiRAGAS keeps correlation only as a starting skeleton, then prunes and orients it with interventional evidence, CRISPR, perturbation, genetics, time, into a directed causal graph with the driver made visible.

Correlation view: dense, undirected, the resistance driver hidden among passengers.
